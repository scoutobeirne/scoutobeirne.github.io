Scout O'Beirne

Freelance consultant

I design learning programs, convenings, and plain-language research that help people imagine and build a more democratic economy. My work sits at the intersection of shared ownership, the future of work, and responsible AI.

I'm on the founding team of Full Spectrum Venture Studio, where we build solidarity-economy businesses from scratch. At UC Berkeley Haas, I translate employee-ownership research for the Institute for Business & Social Impact and lead programming for the Center for Responsible Business's Responsible AI in Society Summit.

Before my MBA at Berkeley Haas ('26), I spent four years at Google designing equity and learning programs that reached about 36,000 people, and served as a building steward with the Alphabet Workers Union. Earlier, I co-founded Flux at IDEO CoLab, where I also led their events and community strategy: facilitating design sprints for over 200 fellows who came from our network companies and students from various degree programs.

My undergraduate degree is in Studies of Women, Gender, and Sexuality from Harvard.

Spring 2026 · Berkeley Haas · Open letter

The Berkeley Haas Doctrine

An open letter asking Haas administration and faculty to address wealth inequality and climate change in the core curriculum, written partly in response to Milton Friedman's 1970 doctrine on the social responsibility of business. I led the student team that wrote it, canvassed professors, and gathered 150+ signatures. It led to the Beyond Capitalism Club and a debate series fostering difficult conversations on business, such as debating shareholder primacy.
